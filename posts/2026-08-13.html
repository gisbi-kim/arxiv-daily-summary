추천 연구주제

Evidence-routed VLA adaptation grid

Compare raw demonstrations, structured demonstrations, reasoning/action tokens, residual online RL, and activation warnings under the same task-shift split; score the first action change and warning lead time before final success.

Coverage-qualified driving replay

Publish driving results with risk slices, rule-triggered command substitutions, factual/counterfactual world-model pairs, and a clear statement of which slices remain uncovered.

Executable 3D memory stress suite

Corrupt target masks, dynamic elements, topology, and metric scale in maps or generated worlds, then evaluate target choice, stop timing, route, base placement, and recovery action rather than render quality alone.
