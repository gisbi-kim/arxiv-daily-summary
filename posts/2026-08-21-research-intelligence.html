Friction / anomaly

Hidden premise

Conceptual move

Move world-action modeling from visual prediction to hierarchical tactile state forecasting.

Evidence trace

  • [Abstract]It forecasts a sequence of future tactile states for each candidate action chunk.
  • [Inference]APRL should compare tactile forecast error against contact failure and recovery behavior.

Mechanism

Falsification frontier

Strong for dexterous and deformable manipulation; weaker for navigation tasks without rich contact.

#3Tier A - abstract-only

SCAPE: Scenario-Conditioned Simulation-Augmented Policy Evaluation

robot policy evaluation - scenario conditioning - sim-to-real bias

Status quo belief

Simulation-augmented robot evaluation often estimates average performance from limited real rollouts and many biased simulated rollouts.

Friction / anomaly

A policy can look good on average while failing in the specific scenario family that matters for deployment.

Hidden premise

Simulation should be conditioned on scenario variables so that real-world evidence audits the failure family, not just the mean.

Conceptual move

Turn policy evaluation into scenario-conditioned estimation under explicit sim-to-real bias.

Evidence trace

  • [Abstract]The paper identifies reliable performance evaluation as a central deployment bottleneck.
  • [Abstract]It contrasts faithful real-world testing with biased scalable simulation.
  • [Inference]APRL should report policy confidence per scenario family instead of one aggregate score.

Mechanism

The abstract frames real testing as faithful but costly, simulation as scalable but biased, and SCAPE as scenario-conditioned evaluation.

Falsification frontier

If scenario conditioning does not change confidence intervals or policy ranking, the method is not exposing hidden deployment risk.

Adversarial read

Choose scenarios where simulation is known to miss contact, lighting, terrain, or occlusion effects.

Transferable thinking tool

Evaluate a policy by the scenario that can break it, not by the average simulator proxy.

Strong for deployable robot policies; weaker for purely offline perception benchmarks.

New signal

Contradiction

Recent VLA adaptation work wants broad transfer, while safety and evaluation papers require narrow evidence gates.

The useful research tension is whether gating, shielding, and scenario conditioning improve deployment without destroying the flexibility that made VLA policies attractive.

Missing axis

The repo still lacks a shared benchmark that connects VLA adaptation, evidence-gated planning, contact-state prediction, and map degeneracy in one episode.

APRL can own that missing axis by designing episodes where the same hidden evidence variable affects planning, contact, and localization decisions.
