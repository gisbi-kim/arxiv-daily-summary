2026-09-09 arXiv Research Intelligence

The September 9 batch makes action authority conditional on evidence freshness. VLA and robot-learning papers ask whether a policy knows where success stops, which memory operation is needed, when a force signal must interrupt an action chunk, and how long reasoning can stay executable on a mobile robot. The safety and systems papers close the loop by asking whether compressed tokens, cached KV states, visual CoT, authority cues, and multimodal guardrails still preserve the exact evidence that should permit, block, or revise an action.

CR-VLA-Force: Learning Control-aware Compliance VLA Model for Robust Contact-rich Robotic Manipulation

contact-rich VLA - force timing - reactive compliance

Status quo belief

Force-aware VLA policies can still execute long action chunks before force feedback changes the command.

Friction / anomaly

The abstract says force-aware VLA models remain weak at precise force tracking and rapid successive adjustment.

Hidden premise

Contact evidence should be allowed to interrupt or reshape an action chunk before unsafe force accumulates.

Conceptual move

Encode force signal sequences with visual-language features and treat compliance as a control-aware VLA capability.

Evidence trace

  • [Abstract]The paper targets action-chunk latency and force-tracking limits in contact-rich manipulation.
  • [Abstract]It introduces Control-aware Compliance VLA for reactive control with force/torque perception.
  • [Inference]APRL should score whether force evidence changes the remaining command before a contact failure completes.

Mechanism

The proposed CC-VLA framework uses multimodal mixture-of-experts features so force and visual-language evidence can jointly shape reactive control.

Falsification frontier

If gains disappear when contact timing, material compliance, or object geometry changes, the method may be fitting familiar force profiles.

Adversarial read

Test delayed force spikes, near-identical visual states with different contact stiffness, and action chunks that must be interrupted mid-execution.

Transferable thinking tool

Treat contact as a permission signal over the next action, not as an auxiliary observation.

Direct for force/torque-equipped manipulation; weaker for tasks without contact sensing or chunked policy execution.

Friction / anomaly

Transferable thinking tool

Direct for active mapping and exploration; less direct for offline reconstruction without a planner in the loop.
