오늘 /new: cs.CV 98 + cs.RO 114 · 197 dedup · 166 ROI papers
오늘의 결론: The 2026-09-21 batch says robot intelligence is moving from final success labels to early evidence timing. VLA reliability papers turn distribution shift, safety stage, proprioceptive deviation, and semantic commitment into signals that must appear before a rollout is unrecoverable. Contact-rich manipulation papers make tactile, force, deformation, and hidden physical properties into predicted state variables rather than passive feedback. Geometry and mapping papers ask what visual, geometric, semantic, or low-light evidence is worth collecting before pose, map, view, or navigation commitments are trusted. Social navigation and recovery papers then make the intervention decision explicit: a robot must know when to ask, slow down, recover, preserve control authority, or change evidence sources. APRL should build assets where every module reports the first observable cue that should change the robot's decision, not only whether the final task succeeded.

추천 연구주제

Early-evidence failure timing benchmark

One manipulation and one navigation suite should log visual shift, proprioceptive onset, tactile forecast, map confidence, social response, and human-query value before final failure.

Contact-state correction authority

Treat tactile, force, deformation, and hidden physical property forecasts as separate channels that can override or delay nominal VLA action chunks.

Evidence-budgeted mapping for robot decisions

Active view, confidence-guided odometry, semantic relocalization, and low-light SLAM should be evaluated by decision evidence gained per compute and time.

Social navigation population-shift stress test

Combine DPed-VLN and PopNavShift-style pedestrian populations with map-language route scaffolds to test whether social compliance survives behavioral shifts.
