추천 연구주제

Action-state contract harness

generated future를 6D pose, contact, object displacement, route feasibility, next-skill readiness로 정규화해 real execution success와 correlation을 측정합니다.

Failure-recovery data protocol

human egocentric recovery와 robot teleop recovery를 같은 failure family에서 수집하고, corrective intent bottleneck과 planner abstraction의 기여를 분리합니다.

Embodiment-aware navigation safety split

same route를 body geometry and transition context별로 반복해 clearance violation, boundary hesitation, unsafe correction, intervention cost를 평가합니다.

Robot silent-failure audit

final success와 별도로 sensor evidence, state estimate, planner rationale, action trace를 taxonomy label로 점검해 right-action-wrong-evidence와 phantom grounding을 잡습니다.
